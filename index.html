Privacy policy

1. What is processed
The builder needs no sign-up and never asks for your name, phone number or email. Your floor plan is handled only in your browser: the file is not uploaded to any server, and every calculation runs on your device. So that a refresh doesn't lose your work, the plan and your progress are kept in this browser for up to 30 days; “Start over with a different plan” deletes them at once.

2. Map and address search
On the map steps (3–5) the map tiles load from OpenStreetMap and Esri servers. The address you type into the search, and the spot where you pin your building, are sent to Esri's address service (or, if it is unavailable, to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim) to find the building and show its address. These services handle requests under their own policies.

3. Cookies and analytics
This site uses no cookies and no visitor tracking or analytics.

4. Contact
Questions about the builder are welcome on Telegram: @annaromeodesign.
